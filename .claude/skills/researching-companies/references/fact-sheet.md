# Fact sheet

Contents: the empty template, then one filled example. The example is synthetic: the company, people and URLs are invented and must never be copied into a real note.

## Empty template

```markdown
## Fact sheet: <Company>, <Role>
Researched <date> from <dossier path or "manual lead">. Tier <1|2>.

### Posting
- Requisition id: <as printed, or "not printed"> (source: <URL>, fetched <date>)
- Deadline as printed: <text> (source: ...)
- Role and team: <text> (source: ...)

### Eligibility and deadlines
- <PhD, clearance or citizenship requirement first, if any> (source: ...)
- <class year, major, work authorization, location> (source: ...)

### Company Information
- <a project from the last 12 months> (source: ...)
- Link to Anant's work: <Main Resume.md bullet or linked project note, by path> or "no connection found"

### People and contacts
Searches run: <each query, or "none">
- <name or "not found">, <title>, <source URL>, found by <query>, email only if verified
- LinkedIn people search for Anant to open himself: the query text <company> <role words>, searched on LinkedIn by hand

### Hiring signal
- <open intern roles or teams a page states> (source: ...) or "none stated"

### Gaps
- <every fact you could not source>

### needs_input
- <questions for Anant, or "none">
```
